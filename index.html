About

The eye behind
the lens

Lucas Lefty is a New York-based sports and editorial photographer with over fifteen years of experience documenting elite athletics at the highest levels of competition. His work is defined by an instinct for stillness within chaos — isolating the single frame that tells the whole story. Clients range from global sporting federations to the world's most prestigious publications.

Career Highlights

  • Summer Olympics, Paris 2024
  • Super Bowl LVIII, Las Vegas
  • FIFA World Cup 2022, Qatar
  • Tour de France — Official Photographer
  • Wimbledon Championships 2019–2024
  • TIME Sportsperson of the Year Cover
